Kinotta launch video: the script

A 76-second vertical video that shows Kinotta doing the one thing it is for: your own footage and your own brand in, a finished reel out, changed by pinning exactly what you want different. It opens with a RayDom talking-head take, raw, then finished.

Exists in the repo's docs. I have not run it. Decided or planned, not built No plan yet

The hook, 0 to 8 seconds

The hook is a before and after in one project. A raw talking-head take plays plain, the RayDom folder lands on Kinotta, and the same take comes back finished in the RayDom look. The drag is the pitch, and it is on screen by second 3.

The full script

The two hook beats above are beats 1 and 2. Voiceover lines are written as a peer talking to the viewer, which matches brand/voice.md. Rewrite any of them in your own words.

An idea with no plan: the inspiration edit

You asked about dragging in an editing style you like. Nothing in the docs does that. The nearest thing is the taste list (docs/plans/2026-10-20-taste-list.md), which the skill reads before every build. A cheap version would be: drop a video you like, and Kinotta writes taste-list entries from it. Treat it as a feature to build first, and add a beat only if it exists.
