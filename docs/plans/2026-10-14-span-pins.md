## Intent

- Problem: A pin lands on one moment. A comment about pacing, such as "tighten this stretch", has to describe where the stretch starts and ends in words.
- Why: Kinotta's promise is to pin, not describe. Pacing is the one kind of feedback that still needs describing. It was split out of `2026-10-13-alternates.md` on 2026-10-13 because it needs neither of that plan's prerequisites and can be built today.
- Proposed outcome: You drag across the Pins row to mark a start and an end, write the comment, and the batch gives the agent both times.
- Affected: comment pins and their carry (`server/core/_internal/types.ts`, `comments.ts`, `carry.ts`), the batch text (`batch.ts`), the Pins row in the Storyboard lanes and the Review timeline (`web/src/Lanes.tsx`, `web/src/review/_internal`), the comments panel, `CONTEXT.md` (Pin gains a span), `skill/kinotta` (how the agent reads a span), tests under `tests/core` and `tests/e2e`. Mockup: step 1 of `docs/mockups/2026-10-13-alternates-in-storyboard.html`.
- Constraints:
  - A click on the Pins row still makes a moment pin. Only a drag makes a span.
  - A span is a pin with an end time. It keeps one number and one hex, like any pin.
  - `DESIGN.md` tokens and the One Light Rule.
  - Keyboard access: a span can be made and adjusted without a pointer.
- Out of scope:
  - Checking that the agent changed nothing outside the span. That arrives with `2026-10-12-shots-as-parts.md`, where "this shot did not change" is a fact on disk.
  - Spans on the frame (a region of the picture over time).
- Open questions:
  - O1: A span that crosses a shot boundary belongs to two shots. Is it listed under both shots in the batch, or once under the first?
  - O2: When a later version retimes the reel, does a carried span keep its length, or stretch to cover the same content?

Decided with the owner in the 2026-10-13 grilling session: span pins get their own plan; a span may run across two shots; no check in the first build that nothing outside the span changed.
