## Intent

- Problem: Footage-reel work needs motion-broll's engine, but it lives outside this repo in a
  standalone skill, and `kinotta check` passes a footage reel whose footage file, transcript or
  shot fields are missing.
- Why: T18 and T22 are the frontier of the footage split. T19 to T21 build on the copied engine,
  T23 on the check.
- Proposed outcome: The Kinotta skill carries its own copy of the engine, its description covers
  b-roll for review in Kinotta, and `kinotta check` fails a broken footage reel with one coded
  line per problem.
- Affected: `skill/kinotta/`, `docs/adr/0001-timing-contract.md`, `server/cli.ts`,
  `tests/core/check.test.ts`, footage test fixtures.
- Constraints: `~/.agents/skills/motion-broll` is not touched. The Geist OFL notice travels with the
  fonts. The check uses the core's public interface only. Code-only reels are checked as before.
- Out of scope: the engine joining the contract (T19, T20), the footage workflow rules (T23), the
  K9 exception (recorded in T23).
- Open questions: none.

## Goal

The acceptance criteria of #21 and #25 met and ticked in `docs/tickets.md`.

## Approach

T18 copies motion-broll's `engine/`, `scripts/`, `templates/`, `examples/` and
`reference/engine-api.md` into `skill/kinotta/` at the same relative paths, so the scripts' own
paths still resolve. Its SKILL.md body becomes `reference/motion-broll.md` (not `SKILL.md`, so no
loader sees a second skill). The Kinotta SKILL.md gains a short footage section pointing at the
material and saying the footage workflow is not written yet. A `.gitattributes` keeps `setup.sh`
LF, since `core.autocrlf=true` would otherwise check it out with CRLF and break bash.

T22 adds footage issues in `server/cli.ts` from the `Version` the core already returns (`footage`,
`transcriptProblem`, each shot's `type`, `line` and `spoken`), test first in `check.test.ts`.

## Steps

1. T18: copy, footage section and description, ADR 0001, `.gitattributes`. Verify by building and
   rendering an example clip from the new place and diffing the standalone skill against a
   snapshot taken before.
2. T22: one failing test per footage problem, then the check. The footage sample passes and
   code-only reels are unchanged.
3. Checks, tick both tickets, summary.

## Risks

- Bundled Playwright browsers are not installed; the render check preloads a shim that launches
  the installed Chrome rather than editing the copied engine.

## Checks to run

`npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run test:e2e`, the engine build and render from
`skill/kinotta/engine/`.
