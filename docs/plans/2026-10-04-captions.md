## Intent

- Problem: A footage reel has no captions, and the owner asked for them on the sample ("can we add captions").
- Why: Talking videos are watched muted; captions are part of the reel the owner signs off.
- Proposed outcome: v1 of a footage reel shows captions over the footage and the b-roll, the owner can pin a
  caption like any element, and the look can be changed in the plan.
- Affected: `skill/kinotta/engine/build.py`, `engine/motion.js`, `scripts/shots.py`, `SKILL.md` sections 5
  and 6, `reference/engine-api.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, tests.
- Constraints: C1 to C6. A caption fix stays local to its section (scenes, F4). Core and editor unchanged.
  Plans without `captions` build byte-identical pages (footage sample drift tests).
- Out of scope: burning captions into an MP4 (no render yet); caption editing in the editor.
- Open questions: none. Look B picked on 2026-10-04 from the sample pass; the owner asked for the look to stay
  changeable.

## Goal

T27's acceptance criteria met; the sample reel rebuilt with captions and checked.

## Approach

- Plan: `"captions": true` or `{ "look": "highlight" | "phrase" | "words", "color": "#FF5A1F" }` (C6, default
  look highlight, default colour the engine's accent), and `"transcript"`, the transcript path relative to the plan.
- `build.py --plan`: groups the transcript into phrases (C2: up to ~6 words, up to 8 to reach a clause end;
  breaks at pauses over 0.3 s and at clause ends once a phrase has 3 words; short gaps held), and writes each as
  a scene `cap-001`… holding one element `caption` (C1) with a span per word and the look on it. Caption CSS
  sits on the page; the look and colour sit on each caption, so changing them changes every section.
- `motion.js` `M.page`: shows caption scenes by time and marks each word said and the current word.
- `shots.py`: one overlay `{ "kind": "CAPTIONS", "name": "Captions" }` over the spoken span (C5).
- Skill: captions on by default for footage reels; fix misheard words in `transcript.json` and list them in the
  hand-over (C4); a caption comment edits the transcript word or the phrase timing. `CONTEXT.md` gains Captions.

## Steps

1. Failing tests: phrase scenes and look in the composed page; plan without captions unchanged; `M.page` word
   marking in a browser; `shots.py` overlay.
2. `build.py`, `motion.js`, `shots.py`.
3. Skill, reference, CONTEXT.
4. Checks; rebuild the sample with captions, `kinotta check`, re-render the preview.

## Risks

- Caption scenes overlap clip scenes; contract checks treat scenes per shot time, so a shot covered by a clip
  and a caption still passes.
- A phrase that straddles a section boundary belongs to both sections' markup, so fixing it changes both.

## Checks to run

`npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run test:e2e`, `kinotta check` on the sample.
