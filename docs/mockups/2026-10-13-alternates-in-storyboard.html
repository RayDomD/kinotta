Three things this screen assumes, yours to overrule

  1. One card per shot, not one per alternate. The grid keeps its shape and the alternates live inside the shot. The other way is three cards for shot 04 on the grid, which shows them side by side without opening anything but breaks the shot count.
  2. Choosing is an edit, not a new version. "Use" changes v3 in place the way a trim does today, and the choice freezes at render. The stricter way is that the choice only travels in the batch and takes effect in v4.
  3. Their Multi-angle is left out. A code-built scene has no camera to move, so there is nothing to map it to.
