Inline alternates: behaviour

Shot 04 has three alternates. Hover over it, or Tab to it, to play the alternate it is showing. Click a dot or press Up/Down to preview another. This does not change the reel. Use puts the shown alternate in the reel as a Working edit. Not this rejects it, with an optional reason. Clicking the still opens the shot sheet.
