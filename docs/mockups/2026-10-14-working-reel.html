The Working screen: how do you know you are on the live reel?

One question. Today you press Save and get a new version. After live edits there is no Save: you just edit, and a version is frozen only when you render a Final or copy comments. The screen has to tell you whether you are on the reel you are working on or on an old frozen version. Both options drop the Save and Discard buttons.

A

"Working" is the top row of the Versions list · my pick

The list you already use to switch versions gets one new row on top. Lit means you are on it. Frozen versions sit below with what froze them.
