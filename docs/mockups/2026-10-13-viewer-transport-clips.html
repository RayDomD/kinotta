Viewer, transport and clips

Three places where their editor does something Kinotta's Review screen does not. Each block shows theirs or today's Kinotta first, then what I would build. A blue outline marks my pick. This changes the layout saved in 2026-10-08-review-media-editor-screen.html, so nothing here is decided until you say so.

V. How much room the picture gets

Today the frame is capped at 36% of the window height (.rv-frame in review.css), whatever the window size. On a 1440 × 900 window that is a 576 × 324 picture. Theirs takes about half the height. The drawings are to scale.
