Storyboard · Working

Drag an effect onto a frame or the alternates strip under it. To add a shot, use Insert shot in the gap between two shots and describe it.

Things to try
  1. Drop Title card on a frame: it is made at once, free.
  2. Click Insert shot in a gap and describe the new shot: it goes to the agent with the next Build.
  3. Drop Section tag on a strip: it becomes an alternate to try.
  4. Click a pin, press Delete; then Ctrl+Z or Undo.
  5. Type a note on an instant pin: it moves to the agent. Build, then answer Keep this note?
