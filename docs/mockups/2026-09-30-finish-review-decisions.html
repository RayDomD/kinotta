Kinotta finish-review decisions, 2026-09-30

Chosen in a ui-preview round after the T17 finish review. The screens below are the options as shown; the choices are listed here.

  • North Star stays "The Light Table".
  • Colour names: set A, Cutting bench (Table, Gate, Slate, Slate Lifted, Title, Caption, Credits, Splice, Frame Line, Tally, Leader, Scrim). Token keys unchanged.
  • After Copy: A, a lit "Sent" button (Tally outline, hex, time) that asks "Copy again?" on hover or focus, and returns to "Copy comments N" when comments change.
  • Phase nav: Review and Picker stay visible and inactive (spec story 66).
  • Enlarged shot: A, the modal sheet stays.
  • Pins: B, a small anchor hex at the exact spot with a numbered comment tag placed outside the element; grid stills show the tag's number at a readable size.
  • Short reels: A, leave the space as it is.
  • Issue list: B, one line with the count and first issue, "Show all" expands; the batch still includes every issue.

After Copy: how should the button look once a batch has been sent?

Ember launch Sent 14:02

At rest it's a Tally outline with the hex, "Sent" and the time. Hovered, it turns back into a normal button that asks "Copy again?". Once a comment changes, it goes back to "Copy comments 4".
